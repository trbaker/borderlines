How each case works

  1. Look. The map zooms to the place. Read the three clues.
  2. Claim. Classify the place: 2 points if right.
  3. Prove it. Pick the clue that best supports your claim: 1 more point.

Stuck? The Field guide is free any time. A hint costs 1 point. You'll finish with one AP-style written question.

For teachers: design and alignment

Time: about 25 minutes. Four short chapters (12 cases), then one three-part free-response question.

Model: case-based inquiry using Claim–Evidence. Each chapter starts with its key terms. In each case students classify a real place on an ArcGIS Online map, then pick the clue that best proves it. Feedback explains the answer and names a common misconception.

CED alignment: Unit 4 Topics 4.1–4.5 and 4.7–4.10. The final question links boundary types to centrifugal forces and devolution.

Evidence of learning: a field report (PNG or PDF) with scores by chapter, the final written answer and the student's self-score.
